i HOW TO READ THE RESULTS
  • Imagery is free Sentinel-2 / Landsat surface reflectance, read directly from public cloud archives (Earth Search on AWS or Microsoft Planetary Computer). Everything is processed in your browser — no Earth Engine and no quota.
  • For each year the clearest scenes are cloud-masked and combined into a per-pixel median water index.
  • Shorelines are vector lines: the threshold contour of the smoothed index, traced with sub-pixel precision (marching squares). Region edges are never drawn as shoreline.
  • Erosion = land that became water; accretion = water that became land, between consecutive years.
  • Rates in m/yr are area-based: area change ÷ mean shoreline length ÷ years.
  • Imagery is not tide-corrected. Changes under ~1 pixel (10 m S2, 30 m Landsat) are within noise — check the true-colour layers.
